# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexual, or disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through repository maintainer contact or GitHub moderation tools. Maintainers should protect reporter privacy and respond proportionately.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related public spaces.
